The setup

Do these once. After that it runs every day.

  1. Get onto Luna

    Sign up at letluna.com. When the payment screen shows up, keep going: it's your 14-day free trial.

  2. Connect your accounts

    Connect LinkedIn, Gmail and anything else you work in. This is how Luna learns your company: what you sell, who buys it, how you talk.

  3. Open a chat in Luna and paste this

    Name the chat something like "ICP outreach" so Claude can find it later.

    Paste into Luna
    You know my company. Build my ICP and positioning for LinkedIn outreach.

Give me:
1. Who to target and why they need us
2. The exact Sales Navigator filters (job title, seniority, industry, geography, company headcount, keywords)
3. Who to exclude
4. A personal connection note for each person, under 300 characters, in my voice

Keep everything in this chat.

  4. Give Claude a browser

    Install the Claude in Chrome extension. If you use Claude Code, start it with claude --chrome. Log in to LinkedIn Sales Navigator and Luna in that same Chrome.

  5. Paste this into Claude

    Paste into Claude
    Open https://letluna.com/dashboard and open my Luna chat called "ICP outreach". Read the ICP, the Sales Navigator filters and the connection notes Luna wrote.

Then open https://www.linkedin.com/sales/search/people?viewAllFilters=true and apply exactly those filters.

For each person who matches, send a connection request with the note Luna wrote for them. Skip anyone already connected or pending. If anything is unclear, ask Luna in that same chat.

Stop at 20 requests and give me a list of who you sent to.

    Keep it around 20 a day. LinkedIn limits how many invites you can send in a week, and a slower pace keeps your account safe.

  6. Watch it run

    Claude reads Luna's plan, opens Sales Navigator and sends each note. Run step 5 again tomorrow for the next batch.
