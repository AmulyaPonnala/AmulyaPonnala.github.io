About

Builder by instinct.
Founder by choice.

I'm Amulya Ponnala — Co-Founder and CTO of FemoraAI, a women's health intelligence platform, and Co-Founder of Pink Parcel LLP, a period kit subscription service we built inside our college hostel at LPU.

I met my co-founder Ramanjit Singh in diploma college. We've been building together for five years. Pink Parcel was where we first saw the problem up close — hundreds of women navigating health conditions alone. FemoraAI is the answer we built from that.

I write all the code — React Native, Java Spring Boot, Python, in-house ML models, AWS cloud infrastructure. Both founders do.

Outside of building, I'm a public speaker and a Google Girl Hackathon alumna. I build in public and document everything — the OS, the metrics, the mistakes.
